# Independent evidence review

Reviewed 2026-11-07 against BASELINE.md, AGENT_GUIDE.md, FOLLOWUPS.md and cross-checks/README.md. Applied audit-lead and language-auditor guidance. Scope: the nine nominated High findings and the three requested duplicate mappings. No product edits, compiler rebuilds, commits or subagents.

**Verdict:** all nine contain a supported concrete failure; no fixture-based false positive found. Retain eight High ratings. Recommend **Medium for AUDIO-01** on the demonstrated impact, and separate its ancestor-limit policy question from its confirmed overlapping-cap defect. Severity is a prioritization judgment, not a contradiction of the reproduction. TYPING-02 is a previously known defect family with a residual reassignment path, not a wholly new issue.
